Privacy policy

Locate and Launch does not collect personal information.

Effective date: October 3, 2026

Information collection

David Nelson, the developer of Locate and Launch, does not collect, store, sell, or share personal information through the app. The app does not send your search terms, source contents, or action history to the developer.

No analytics or tracking

Locate and Launch does not use advertising trackers or third-party analytics to monitor your activity.

Data you use in the app

The app uses the files, sources, settings, and action configurations you choose to provide so that it can search text and run actions. This data is used on your device and is not collected by the developer. Storage or syncing provided by iOS or a file provider is governed by that provider’s policies.

Connections you choose

If you load a source from a URL or an S3-compatible service, the app connects to that service to retrieve the content. If you run an action, the selected values may be passed to the website, app, Shortcut, or server you chose.

Those services may receive information needed to complete your request, such as your IP address, credentials you supply, or values included in an action. Their privacy policies apply to those interactions. This information is not collected by the developer of Locate and Launch.

Support requests

If you choose to contact the developer through GitHub, any information you include is provided voluntarily and used to respond to your request. GitHub issues are public and are subject to GitHub’s privacy policy. Please avoid posting sensitive information.

Changes to this policy

This page will be updated if the app’s privacy practices change. The effective date above will reflect the latest update.

Questions

For questions about this policy, visit the support page.
